# The Plata Clan at home: lines in their hideouts. Spoken as speech lines, so 90 characters at most.
# Members sleep standing up in silver husks (shed shells) and burst out when a stranger comes near.
# hideout.wake: a sleeper bursting out. Groggy, put out, territorial; the hideout is their stone and you broke in.
# hideout.elder: the elder asleep over the vault's Clan Coffer wakes (a Molt Warden, or below level 20 a Shardcaster).
#   Slow, heavy, proprietary about the coffer; the clan's hoard and its patience.
# hideout.reseal: a sleeper with nobody left to fight crawls back into its husk. Smug, sleepy, unbothered.
# A line may start with {scuttler}, {shardcaster} or {warden}. Placeholders: {player}, {rank}.
