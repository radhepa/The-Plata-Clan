A Village Friends add-on

The Plata Clan

Out in the wild, a lone villager waits by the road. Talk to them and they shed the disguise: the Plata Clan, sworn to the silverfish, which hides in plain stone, can't be stamped out and swarms the moment it is disturbed.

We are in the stone.

The encounter

The Molt

The traveler is a real Village Friends resident with a name and a face, belonging to no village. Their greeting comes through Village Friends' own conversation window. Whatever you answer, the window closes and the disguise drops in about two seconds. These are frames from the game.

Three ranks, one body

The ranks

Every Plata wears the clan's costume on the same body Village Friends draws its residents with: segmented silver plate like a silverfish's back, a banded face plate, two feelers, and three tail cords for the silverfish's three tail strands.

{{RANKS_0}}

Scuttler

The footsoldier, and most of the clan. Twin fang knives held reverse-grip: quick flurries, a lunging stab, backflips, and circling just out of reach. From level 20 they call silverfish out of the ground.

{{RANKS_1}}

Shardcaster

Keeps you at arm's length behind fans of sharpened silver shards that sting and slow. Close in and they kick, flip away, or burrow off and come up somewhere else.

{{RANKS_2}}

Molt Warden

The elder enforcer, from level 20. The Silvertail is a segmented whip built like a giant silverfish: a long sweeping lash, and a slam that cracks a ring in the ground. Step out before it erupts.

In motion

Moves

Twenty clips in Village Friends' animation format, posed by the same rules as its residents. Every hit lands on the beat its clip is keyed for. Each rank can burrow: they dive in a burst of grit and travel unseen under the ground, and a running trail of cracking dust and a short tremor give them away before they come up under you.

Always a fight

Levels at your level

A Plata's level comes from the player who found them: the character level from Village Friends RPG if it's installed, otherwise a power level read off armor, weapon, health and experience. Health and damage come from the player's own numbers, so the fight is neither a pushover nor a slaughter at any stage.

  • Under 35% health a Plata of level 15 or more molts: they burrow twice in a row and fight faster.
  • The clan counts the silverfish you've killed. The more you've crushed, the more often they come for you.
What they leave

Loot

Emeralds and Silver Scales every time. Sometimes a Plata Fang, a quick reverse-grip knife you can also craft from scales. Rarely the Plata Mask: wear it and disguised Plata take you for kin and whisper clan gossip instead of fighting.

Where they live

Hideouts: in the stone

The clan lives the way the silverfish taught it, inside the stone and under something nobody looks at twice. A Scale Den hides under a traveler's camp; a Silver Warren under a ruined toll-house, with a cistern that is really a spiral stair twenty blocks down. Each has a lookout up top: a disguised Plata, happy to chat until they aren't. Both come in six regional styles and keep well away from villages and homesteads.

Every Warren branches differently from the Molting Hall, where the clan's mark runs the length of the floor. Ribbed tunnels, built like the segments of a silverfish's back, lead to the Shell Gallery, the Sparring Pit, the Scale Forge, the Brood, the Shrine of the First Molt and the Storeroom. Behind the hall, the vault: an elder asleep over the Clan Coffer. The walls are stronghold brick, and some of it is infested.

Tread softly

Sleepers in silver husks

Down below the clan sleeps standing up in silver husks, their own shed shells. Come within sight and a few blocks and a husk cracks open: a Plata springs out at your level, and the husks around it follow, one after another. Sneak and you can creep much closer. Never more than three are up at once; the rest wait their turn. Leave, and they crawl back into their shells, which seal behind them.

In the modpack

Gets along with everything

  • Village Friends 2.26+ is required and hooked from outside: no Village Friends file is changed. The disguise talk uses its conversation window, and the Molt plays through its animation system. Its guards treat a revealed Plata as a threat.
  • Village Friends RPG is optional: your character level sets theirs, and every Plata counts toward the Vermin family in the Bestiary.
  • Hideouts keep clear of Village Friends villages and homesteads, using their own placement that skips any structure set that isn't loaded.
  • Not-So-Vanilla Mobs and Turning Seasons share no ids and no mixin targets with this mod.
